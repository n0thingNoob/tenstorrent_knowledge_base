## Summary snippet

While RISC-V-based accelerators were initially designed with artificial intelligence applications in
mind, they are increasingly being recognized as promising platforms for high performance scientific
computing. In this work, we present three strategies for scaling an $N$-body code across multiple
Tenstorrent Wormhole accelerators based on the RISC-V architecture. We assess the performance of
these approaches by measuring both the execution time and the energy consumption required to
complete a representative simulation, ultimately identifying the configuration that offers the most
favorable balance between efficiency and performance.
